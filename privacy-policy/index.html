Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

Welcome to ThoughtsToPen (“we,” “our,” or “us”). Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used, and disclosed when you visit and interact with our website at https://thoughtstopen.com.

1. Information We Collect

A. Log Files

Like most standard website servers, ThoughtsToPen utilizes log files. The information inside the log files includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP)
  • Date and time stamps
  • Referring and exit pages
  • Number of clicks and page navigation paths

This data is used solely to analyze trends, administer the site, track user movement around the site, and gather broad demographic information. Log data is not linked to any personally identifiable information.

B. Voluntary Information

If you contact us directly via our Contact Form or leave comments on our articles, we may receive additional information such as your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

ThoughtsToPen uses cookies to store information about visitor preferences, record user-specific information on which pages the user accesses or visits, and customize web page content based on visitors’ browser type or other information that the visitor sends via their browser.

You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser settings. For detailed information regarding cookie management with specific web browsers, visit the respective browser websites (e.g., Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).

3. Google AdSense & Advertising Partners

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Google is a third-party vendor that uses cookies to serve ads on ThoughtsToPen.

  • DoubleClick DART Cookie: Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visits to ThoughtsToPen and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page at www.aboutads.info.
  • Third-Party Ad Networks: Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons in their respective advertisements and links that appear on ThoughtsToPen. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. ThoughtsToPen has no access to or control over these cookies used by third-party advertisers.

4. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to monitor and analyze web traffic and understand how visitors engage with our content.

Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit this site, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to coming to this site. We use this information strictly to improve site performance and content relevance. Google’s ability to use and share information collected by Google Analytics about your visits to this site is restricted by the Google Analytics Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. GDPR & European Economic Area (EEA) / UK Privacy Rights

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have specific data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Object to Processing: You have the right to object to our processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request that we transfer data we have collected to another organization or directly to you.
  • Consent Management: For visitors from the EEA and UK, we employ a Google-certified Consent Management Platform (CMP) allowing you to grant, customize, or revoke consent for cookies and personalized ads at any time.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are entitled to:

  • Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected about them.
  • Request deletion of any personal data collected.
  • Request that their personal data not be sold or shared.

ThoughtsToPen does not sell personal information to third parties.

7. Children’s Information (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting children’s privacy online is especially important to us. ThoughtsToPen does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or guardian believes that ThoughtsToPen has in its database the personally identifiable information of a child under the age of 13, please reach out to us immediately via our Contact Form and we will use our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to external websites, articles, and documentation. If you click on a third-party link, you will be directed to that site. Note that these external sites are not operated by us. We strongly advise you to review the Privacy Policy of those websites as we have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

9. Consent and Changes to This Privacy Policy

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the Last Updated date at the top.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding our Privacy Policy or data practices, please reach out to us through our secure contact form:

  • Website: https://thoughtstopen.com
  • Contact Form: Send us a message via Contact Form
