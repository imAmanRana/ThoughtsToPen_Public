About Thoughts To Pen

Welcome to ThoughtsToPen — a digital garden and technical publication dedicated to distilling complex ideas in software engineering, algorithmic trading, personal finance, and psychology into clear, actionable insights.

✍️ The Story Behind “Thoughts To Pen”

In software development and in life, clarity comes from articulation. The philosophy behind ThoughtsToPen is simple:

“If you can’t explain it simply, you don’t understand it well enough.”

This space began as a personal journal to document technical architectures, software engineering deep-dives, and financial models. Over time, it has evolved into a comprehensive knowledge hub aimed at helping engineers, retail traders, and curious minds level up their craft without the noise or superficial summaries common across the web.

Whether it is breaking down a newly released Java specification, designing a zero-brokerage algorithmic execution engine, or modeling long-term compound growth in a TFSA, every article is written with a depth-first, beginner-friendly approach.
